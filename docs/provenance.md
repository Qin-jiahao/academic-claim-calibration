This project is an independent bilingual synthesis and safety-oriented rewrite by Qin-jiahao of ideas from two public research-writing projects. ## 改写范围 / Adaptation scope

本项目新增或重写的内容包括：

- 双语 Skill 输入、输出、工作流程和质量检查；
- 将“防御性写作”限制为无信息修辞，禁止选择性报告；
- 将 Oral 论文学习做法改写为一到三项可选择的审查问题；
- 对摘要级、全文级和一般性建议作阅读层级区分；
- 增加作者选择、来源轨迹和“最多三项改进”的限制；
- 增加静态验证和未核验状态说明。

New or substantially rewritten content includes:

- Bilingual Skill inputs, outputs, workflow, and quality checks;
- A narrow definition of defensive writing that prohibits selective reporting;
- A one-to-three-practice review framework based on the Oral-paper learning ideas;
- Reading-level distinctions for abstracts, full text, and general guidance;
- Author-choice, source-trail, and “at most three improvements” constraints;
- Static validation and explicit unverified-status notes.
